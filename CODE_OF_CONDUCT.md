# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when disagreeing.
- Separate evidence from speculation.
- Make performance and safety claims reproducible.
- Welcome good-faith questions and first-time contributors.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's logs, paths, prompts, credentials, or model outputs without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate credential exposure, deceptive benchmark claims, or attempts to bypass the project's safety boundaries are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact channels. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant and is intentionally concise for the v0.1 developer preview.
